Payment Policy

Transparent and straightforward pricing aligned with your success.

Competitive Fee Structure

At Ilyas Cargo Services, we maintain a straightforward and transparent payment policy that aligns with our commitment to client satisfaction. Our approach emphasizes clear fee structures without hidden surprises.

We operate using a competitive fee model that charges a fee of 5% of the gross profit from each load. This percentage is calculated on gross profit from goods transport rather than total revenue. This distinction means we prioritize your earnings and profitability maximization.

By taking a percentage of profit rather than flat fees, we align our interests with yours, making our success dependent on your success.

Transparency is Key

We believe that clear communication about costs fosters trust and builds strong relationships. Our 5% fee structure avoids unexpected charges or ambiguous pricing structures.

Before work begins, you will understand exactly how fees are calculated, enabling proper budgeting and financial planning for your operations.

Maximizing Your Earnings

Our profit-based fee empowers you to maximize your earnings. We aim to enhance your overall financial success, not just handle dispatching. Our experienced team applies deep industry knowledge to ensure that you receive the best possible outcome from each load.

Efficiency in Operations

Our performance-based fee structure motivates us to continuously enhance our operations. We invest in tracking technology, leverage data analytics for route optimization, and maintain strong communication with all stakeholders to deliver the best results.

A Partnership Built on Trust

Our payment policy fosters a partnership built on trust where both parties can thrive through aligned interests. When you succeed, we succeed – and that's the foundation of everything we do at Ilyas Cargo Services.
